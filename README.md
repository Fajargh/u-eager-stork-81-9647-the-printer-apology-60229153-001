## The entry

An apology note from an office printer to the people who keep jamming it, feeding it cheap paper and swearing at it:

> Sorry I jammed again. In my defence, you fed me paper thinner than your excuses - and then you swore at me.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

This writes the one-line apology to `/output/entry.txt`.
